Clipper

Clipper is a private desktop tool. Its owner uses it to upload their own short gaming clips to their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

Clipper uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the owner's channel and set their title, description and paid-promotion label. It doesn't read, collect or share anyone else's data.
